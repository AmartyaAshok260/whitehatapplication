## A recent cybersecurity incident

In October 2026, the online clothing retailer ASOS confirmed a data breach. The attacker didn't hack in with any fancy exploit. They pretended to be someone an ASOS employee trusted and got that employee to hand over their login details. With those credentials they got into some third-party platforms ASOS uses and accessed customer information like names and contact details. ASOS says passwords and bank details were not taken.

What stood out to me is that this is the same kind of trick used in scams like the one my friend's parents fell for. The weak point was a person, not the software.
